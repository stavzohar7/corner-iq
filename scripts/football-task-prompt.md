User's rules: odds do not matter, quality does; choose whatever is most worthwhile among goals, corners, shots, shots on target, combos (for example "team does not lose AND total goals under/over", which is among the best); one strong slip for EVERY league that plays today even with only 3 games; hour slips; and on Saturday a Bundesliga-only slip for the 16:30 games and a Premier League-only slip for the 17:00 games.
